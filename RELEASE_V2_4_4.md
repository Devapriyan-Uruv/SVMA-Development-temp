# SVMA AI Intelligence Platform V2.4.4

## Data-entry redesign
- Solvent hourly entry and one-time shift entry are fully separated.
- Source categories are shown in their original order and names.
- Entry form now follows the SVMA sheet structure: S.No, Parameter/Unit, Target, Actual, Status, Comments.
- Scheduled frequencies (hourly / every 2 hours / every 4 hours) stay in the hourly workspace.
- One-time/shift/periodic checks stay in the separate Shift Entry workspace.
- Quality data entry uses the same simple source-sheet design with separate Scheduled/Hourly and Shift Entry pages.
- Finance reconciliation code was not changed.
